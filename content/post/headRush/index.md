## Head Rush

i think guilt is the strongest emotion i’ve learned how to feel. shame is the mover + shaker when i look back and can’t understand my own behaviour. when you asked me why someone had my ID, i wondered if you knew already. if you had an inkling, if you were softly asking the question your mouth was too kind to form. i hid from you and then followed it up with a lie that i layered, and i’m sorry. there was no woman walking behind me who had spent enough time analyzing it to determine it had expired. there was only the woman at the vape store who asked for a second ID to confirm against.


the entirety of my seattle stay, the thought never crossed my mind. beforehand, i wondered if the independence would lure me back into the habit, especially without anybody to check me for 3 months. but i was starting a new life + chose to leave certain things behind. i don’t think i touched another until the one i bought in may, after which i promised you never have to worry again.


my first night here was so overwhelming. though i tried to slow down + make peace with that decision, my mind moved so fast. the first piece of writing i churned out here was a list of all the goals i want to accomplish next year. i’ve been writing + rewriting this list for weeks already, but sometimes i see myself as an idea engineer, spending more time planning than i’d ever come close to spending in actual doing. i was more stressed than excited about this free time, trying to premeasure my output here.


i know you want me to spit it out rather than stall with my sob story, but my guilt still has the wheel. stepping in now.


more often than not, when i vape, i do it wrong and don’t actually achieve a head rush, the only reason i do it. i’m not skilled at this shit. when i do get the head rush, though, it feels so good. so damn good. other than weed, which never affected me in any notable way, i haven’t tried any drugs, so this is kind of the highest rush i’ve ever felt. not to detract, but genuinely only maybe second to receiving head.


the rush lasts only 20–30 seconds, but it is so intense i can’t keep my eyes open. sometimes i’ll tilt my head back + have to shield my closed eyes w my hand because i get so intensely stimulated. after the high fades, i get this euphoric sense of being unstoppable. a rich feeling of “yeah you’re that bitch” possesses me, and that’s a different type of high that my ambitious self also appreciates.


yesterday was not bad. i hit it once, felt that pride, and then wrote for 5 hours. i kept looking at it but didn’t need to pick it up again because i’d reached such a flow state. i hit it once again at the end of the night, then left it in my studio so it wouldn’t interfere w my nighttime/morning routine.


today i chased the high too hard. i’d cough up because i did it wrong, + kept trying, chasing a high with only about a 1 in 4 success rate. i can’t do it immediately back to back, so i’d wait ten minutes and try again. the thing is, it affects your body in little, ugly ways. there are the physical, like the phlegm i produce and the dryness of my tongue, but there are also the mental, like the disappointment in myself for not getting to work until a high is achieved. i was fiending today, and my dissatisfaction + subsequent lack of productivity cycled viciously. a lot of shame rattled in my lungs, my breath, my heart rate, my rapid fingers that were prioritizing poison over my poetry.


before the first time i ever picked one up, i did tons of research to confirm that moderation wouldn’t fuck up my life. today, after a day of feeling like a different kind of shit, i researched with a different set of questions.


my eyes become incredibly focused when i exhale. i randomly start noticing tiny, delicate details around me. today my first high illustrated that there are three broken chunks of the cork at the edge of my desk. the drywall has tiny circular paint blobs along the bottom, spaced out unevenly. the lamp on my bookshelf has two tiny scratches at its base.


research unveiled that nicotine was fucking with exactly the things i’d been noticing: my attention, my thirst, my blood flow, my heart rate.


none of this science was exciting to discover, which i imagine you feel. you probably don’t want to hear about this shit anyways, and i’m glad it will never be relevant to you. i don’t want it to be relevant to me either. i’m sorry to walk down this road with you again, and i know i promised last time was the “last” time. i’m so disappointed in myself, and you never do anything to disappoint me, so i’m sorry.


aamz and i used to tag team this shit and eventually made a mutual pact to stop. we went back and forth over the years breaking it, but there were fewer slip ups over the last year or 2. i didn't tell her about may, partly due to shame, but mostly out of fear that i'd normalize the idea to her again.


accordingly, it was disheartening a few weeks back to catch her by accident on facetime. she shared that she’d had it for months, though her frequency of use had decreased. she promised there was no risk of addiction, but all i heard was that i was alone in this. we hadn’t really checked in on each others’ statuses, mostly because i thought it was no longer relevant, but the newfound knowledge had me considering it again.


when she came over the next day, i immediately asked if she brought it, but she hadn’t—true to her word (if it was even true), it was only for special occasions. the day after, though, she did bring it to the wedding. i only took a couple hits, but i couldn’t remember how to get a high. i gave up, but there was now an itch in my mind to remember.


the real kicker was actually the bonfire. yes, that one. one of the girls’ purses had fallen open on the picnic mat, and 2 of them had fallen out. though i wouldn’t hit it in a group setting, she’d asked me for a ride earlier, so i was excited at the prospect of hitting it on the way home.


the lore goes a bit deeper here too, though. that girl had had a much more aggressive addiction, and for years she has also made tons of claims that she had quit. between her and aamz, i reflected upon myself as a past user, and i suppose i internalized that you never really escape from it. these girls just created internal permission for myself to return to the same vice, over and over, because it happens to the best of us.


though i doubt it’s where your mind is going, i am not blaming my women or judging their choices. i just want you to understand the inner workings of my brain. actually, i just wanted to understand them. and this long-winded confession does feel a bit healing. every time i write about my disappointment in myself, i somehow also find a little extra grace.


you can process and respond to this whenever and however you see fit. you want me to always tell you these things, and just as last time, my purchase came with the decision not to. however, as i tried and failed to achieve a head rush three, four, five times today, because i did bring it into my bedroom, i gave up and let my head hit the pillow. and i was so tired, i could’ve knocked out instantly.


but i missed you, and i wanted to think about you.


my lips were dry, chapped by the mystical thing nicotine was doing to my insides, and i imagined kissing you.


i imagined cuddling with you, but that sensation wasn’t strong enough, so i imagined you eating me, and i imagined that sensation, and that’s when my eyes bolted open and my heart started racing, contradicting the state of sleep i was seconds from reaching. it’s going to sound so weird now that i’m describing it rather than feeling it, but that realization that i was chasing a high that is humanly achievable was terrifying.


because i do know more fruitful highs—making my instagram blog after a day with my friends, my mom playing with my hair as i lay on her lap, my brother telling me his friends were gassing me up. getting that first head rush makes me feel like there is no greater satisfaction in the world, and i’ll go days, weeks clinging to it. i asked for the smallest possible size in the store and then i asked for one higher.


i’ve just smeared the thing in pizza oil and thrown it in the trash, so it will not be seeing me again. the main reason i wanted to write this, though, is so i remember. i feel like every time i quit, i have all these beliefs that do not stick because somehow my memory has wiped. i’ve wasted $40 between the may stint + this one, which isn’t breaking the bank, but i need to make $1M next year.


you have always shown me so much grace. i have so much gratitude for the fact. in this case specifically, i kind of wish you would be mean. i wish you would tell me it makes you sick, disgusted, irritated, hurt, untrusting. i wish you could say things to make me hate myself more. but i will accept your grace too, because i need this change to be for me first, and then for you.
